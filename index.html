About

I am an NSF postdoc (NSF-MSPRF) and Hill Assistant Professor at Rutgers University. I obtained my Ph.D. from Brown University, where I was grateful to be advised by Jeff Hoffstein and Junehyuk Jung. My thesis was on quantum unique ergodicity for modular forms on cocompact Riemann surfaces arising from quaternion algebras.

My primary areas of interest are analytic number theory and AI for math. In number theory I am interested in the spectral theory of automorphic forms, L-functions, and hyperbolic geometry. On the AI side I am interested in the formalization (and auto-formalization) of mathematics, and in neurosymbolic AI.

I graduated in 2020 from Georgia Tech with a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics under the advisement of Matt Baker.
